Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

SafeDish ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Name and email address (for account creation)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Date of birth, gender, height, and weight
  • Nutrition goals and preferences

Usage Data:

  • Food photos you upload for analysis
  • Meal logs and nutritional data
  • Weight entries and progress photos
  • Exercise logs

Technical Data:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage analytics
  • Crash reports and error logs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Analyze food photos using AI to estimate nutritional content
  • Track your nutrition progress and goals
  • Send you notifications and updates (with your consent)
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Respond to customer support requests

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase for data storage, which provides:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted data at rest
  • Regular security audits
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, AI analysis)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger or acquisition

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Disable certain data collection features

To exercise these rights, go to Profile → Delete Account or contact us at support@safedish.app.

7. Children's Privacy

SafeDish is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In (for authentication)
  • Apple Sign-In (for authentication)
  • RevenueCat (for subscription management)
  • Supabase (for data storage)
  • OpenAI (meal and ingredient photo analysis)
  • Google Gemini (backup meal and ingredient photo analysis)
  • n8n, hosted by Hostinger (routes photos to the AI providers)

Photos you choose to analyze are sent to these AI providers only to estimate nutrition and ingredients. We do not use your photos or health data for advertising, and we do not sell them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
